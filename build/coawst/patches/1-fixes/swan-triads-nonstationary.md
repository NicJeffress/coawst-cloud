## In short

`TRIAD` switches on SWAN's triad wave-wave interactions, which move energy
from the spectral peak to its harmonics in shallow water, before and during
breaking. SWAN computes them only

```fortran
!  *** compute the 3 wave-wave interactions if in each geographical gridpoint
!  *** a continuous spectrum is present, i.e., after first iteration
IF ( ICUR .EQ. 0 .OR. ITER .GT. 1 ) THEN
```

that is, in every iteration when there are no currents, and from the second
iteration on when there are. The guard is meant for stationary runs, whose
first iteration starts from an empty spectrum.

A coupled run always has currents (ROMS sends them; `ICUR` = 1), and
nonstationary SWAN does **one iteration per time step** unless `NUM ...
NONSTAT mxitns` asks for more. So in a coupled run with SWAN's defaults the
triad term was never computed at all: the `TRIAD` line had no effect, and
nothing said so.

## How it was found

In an options campaign on an idealized fringing reef (Reef_Ideal_A15 with a
dense canopy, 24 h coupled runs), two SWAN iterations per step instead of
one cut the breaking dissipation across the reef by 73 %, the wave set-up in
the lagoon by 41 % and the transport over the crest by 51 %, while halving
SWAN's time step changed nothing. SWAN's `REDTRIAD` output was exactly zero
everywhere with one iteration and up to 36 W/m2 with two, and a run without
the `TRIAD` line was bit-identical to the run with it.

## triads_nonstationary: the change

A nonstationary time step starts from the previous step's spectrum, which
is as continuous as the spectrum after a stationary first iteration. The
guard gets a third case:

```fortran
!       Project patch: also in the first iteration of a nonstationary
!       time step, which starts from the previous step's spectrum
!       (otherwise TRIAD is never computed in coupled runs with one
!       iteration per step)
        IF ( ICUR .EQ. 0 .OR. ITER .GT. 1 .OR. NSTATC .EQ. 1 ) THEN
```

`NSTATC` (`SWCOMM3`) is 1 in nonstationary computations. Stationary runs,
runs without currents and runs without `TRIAD` go through exactly the same
code as before. The very first time step of a run starting from rest has an
empty spectrum, as a run without currents always has, and the triad
routines handle that (no energy, no transfer).

## How the test works

Reef_Ideal_A15_veg (coupled, periodic, waves ramped to Hs 1 m over 30 min),
60 min, ROMS 1 x 2 + 2 SWAN, built with the whole series without and with
this patch:

- *bug*: before, `REDTRIAD` is zero at every point and output time;
- *fixed*: after, `REDTRIAD` is nonzero on the reef and the wave field changes;
- *unchanged*: after, with the `TRIAD` line removed, every SWAN and ROMS
  output is bit-identical to before (with `TRIAD`): the patch changes nothing
  else, and before really ran without triads.

## Engine test results

Run 2026-11-02 on the control VM (4 vCPUs, ROMS 1 x 2 + 2 SWAN, 60 min,
output every 10 min), the whole series without and with this patch:

| Check | Result |
|---|---|
| *bug*: `REDTRIAD` before | **0** nonzero values at any point and output time |
| *fixed*: `REDTRIAD` after | nonzero at **15330** point-times, largest 28.5 W/m2 (on the reef); Hs differs from before by up to **0.105 m** |
| *unchanged*: after without `TRIAD` vs before with it | **37 output files bit-identical** (every SWAN BLOCK file and the ROMS history, averages and diagnostics) |
| wall time | before 302 s, after 294 s, after without `TRIAD` 317 s (machine noise; the triad term is cheap) |

Over a whole day (24 h, same case, a dense canopy everywhere, 10 + 6
processes), switching triads on through a second SWAN iteration (the only
way before this patch) cut the breaking dissipation across the reef by 73 %,
the lagoon set-up by 41 % and the transport over the crest by 51 %. Runs with
the patch and one iteration per step are the next measurement.

## Who is affected

Every coupled COAWST run with `TRIAD` in `swan.in` and one SWAN iteration per
time step (SWAN's nonstationary default), and every nonstationary SWAN run
with currents read from files: their triads were off. Coupled runs with two
or more iterations per step had triads from the second iteration on; they
change less. Runs without `TRIAD` are untouched.
